Text2Curl · AI searchAsk in plain language which API responses talk about something.

1. Provider

The key stays inside the extension only: web pages and other extensions cannot read it, it is never synced to your Google account, and it is sent only to the Base URL above (from the extension's background worker, not from the page). By default it is kept in memory only and erased when you close the browser; tick “Remember” to keep it on this computer.

2. Model

Press “Load models”.
Embedding, image, audio, speech and moderation models are hidden automatically: they cannot read API responses and answer. Models that support structured output (JSON schema) are listed first: their answers are type-safe and always parse.

3. Save & test

Privacy: when you run an AI search, short excerpts of the captured responses on that page (and their URLs, with secrets masked) are sent to the provider above. Headers and cookies are never sent. Do not use AI search on pages with data you are not allowed to share.
